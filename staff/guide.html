SAW walker: staff guide

How to switch the exhibit on and off, use the staff remote and fix common problems. The walker runs on its own: no internet and no computer needed.

Every day

Switching on

  1. Switch on the walker's power at the wall (or plug it in).
  2. Wait about 2 minutes. The screen shows the tour and it starts by itself, with the handle lights and the voice.
  3. Nothing else to do. The tour repeats all day. Visitors can tap the buttons along the bottom of the screen.

Switching off

  1. Open the staff remote on your phone (scan the QR code above), stand near the walker and tap Connect to the walker
  2. Enter the staff password and tap Shut down, then confirm. The handle lights flash red.
  3. Wait until the walker's screen goes dark (about 15 seconds).
  4. Switch off at the wall.
No phone to hand? You can switch off at the wall directly. The walker is set up to cope with this, but a proper shut down is safer for its memory card. Please avoid switching off during the first 2 minutes after switching on.

The staff remote

The staff remote works over Bluetooth, within a few metres of the walker. It needs the staff password (ask the research team, it is not written here).

PhoneWhat to use
AndroidChrome. Allow Bluetooth and "Nearby devices" when asked.
iPhone / iPadThe free Bluefy browser from the App Store (Safari does not support Bluetooth).
LaptopChrome or Edge with Bluetooth switched on.
ButtonWhat it does
StatusShows how long it has been running and whether the lights, voice and screen are working.
Restart tourStarts the tour again from the beginning (takes a few seconds).
Mute narration / Unmute narrationTurns the tour's voice off or on. Visitors can still ask questions with "Talk to me".
Restart walkerRestarts the whole walker. Back after about 2 minutes.
Shut downShuts the walker down safely before switching off at the wall.

After 5 wrong passwords the remote locks itself for 5 minutes.

If something is wrong

What you seeWhat to do
Black screen, or the screen is frozenStaff remote: Restart tour. If that does not help: Restart walker. Without a phone: switch off at the wall, wait 10 seconds, switch on.
Error page ("Aw, Snap!")Wait 1–2 minutes: the walker restarts the screen by itself. Otherwise as above.
No voiceCheck whether narration is muted (red speaker button at the top of the screen, or Status on the remote). Tap it or use Unmute narration.
Handle lights stay offRestart walker. If they are still off, tell the research team.
Screen upside downWait a few seconds, it corrects itself. If not, restart the walker.
"Talk to me" button missingIt appears a minute or two after switching on. If it stays missing, restart the walker.
The "Laser" or "Posture" part of the tour is skippedA sensor is not connected. Do not open the walker; tell the research team.
The walker does not start at allTell the research team. If you have been given a spare memory card, they may ask you to swap it.

Please

  • Do not unplug or move the cables, the laser sensor near the floor or the camera.
  • The walker is an exhibit: please do not let visitors use it as a walking aid or lean their weight on it.
  • Clean the touch screen with a dry or slightly damp soft cloth only.
  • The camera image and voices are processed on the walker and never recorded or sent anywhere.
